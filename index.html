Bind planner

On a narrow screen, swipe across the board to see every key.

Spells

Drag a spell from the tray onto a key, or pick a layer and click a key. The tray holds only the spells that are not on the board: dropping one on a key moves it there and it leaves the tray, so nothing sits on two keys. Clear a key to send its spell back. The five dots under each key show which layers have something on it, with the selected layer ringed. Alt is kept for mounts, food, potions and other items, so no spell is put there. G, T, S, B and Space stay utility keys, and the interface panels sit on the client's own default keys: P Spellbook, N Talents, L Quest log, O Social, M Map, H Honor, K Skills, U Reputation and Esc, with Shift+I, Shift+P, Shift+M and Shift+Space for the pet book, pets, battle map and score screen.
